# QB Intel - Death to Nottighams

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Confidence starters

### Josh Allen - BUF
- **Role:** Clear weekly QB1 with elite rushing and passing upside.
- **Why it matters:** Not a waiver target unless the roster is desperate; he is a must-start.

### Jalen Hurts - PHI
- **Role:** Dual-threat QB1 with rushing potential that adds weekly safety.
- **Why it matters:** Great weekly floor and ceiling when healthy.

### Patrick Mahomes - KC
- **Role:** starter-level QB with matchup-driven upside.
- **Why it matters:** Useful if you need a streaming play, but not usually a priority add.

## Streaming / backup notes

### Baker Mayfield - TB
- **Role:** Streamable QB with passing-volume upside in favorable matchups.
- **Why it matters:** Good plug-in option if your starter is out or your bench is thin.

### Daniel Jones - IND
- **Role:** Streaming option with rushing floor.
- **Why it matters:** Useful if the offense and role are stable enough to produce a solid QB2 week.

## Notes

- QB adds should be driven by starting need, not a full roster churn.
- Check the relevant matchup and injuries before spending waiver resources on a quarterback.
