# Draft Strategy Core — Yahoo Guillotine Auction League

## Philosophy

This league combines three unusual factors that don't exist together in the ESPN "click clack" league, so ESPN-league instincts don't transfer directly:

1. **Auction, not snake** — value is set by real-time bidding against 17 other budgets, not by draft position. Being "right" about a player's rank matters less than being right about their *price relative to the room*.
2. **TE gets full PPR while everything else is half-PPR** — a genuine structural premium on elite pass-catching TEs (see `positional-value-tiers.md`).
3. **Guillotine elimination, no trades** — favors floor/depth over ceiling/stars-and-scrubs, and means waiver/FAAB management is your only in-season lever (see `guillotine-strategy.md`).

## Draft-day priorities

1. **Set price ceilings before the draft starts**, not during — know roughly what you're willing to pay for each tier (elite TE, RB1/WR1, solid starter, depth) so bidding-room adrenaline doesn't push you past your own math. Use `auction-budget-strategy.md`'s allocation framework as the starting point.
2. **Prioritize roster balance over a stacked top-3.** The elimination format punishes a thin bench harder than a normal season-long league would.
3. **Actively decide your TE plan** — pay up for a real target-hog, or fully punt and stream. The danger zone is landing in the middle: overpaying for a TE who doesn't have the volume to justify the premium.
4. **Bye-week awareness matters more than usual** — a bad single week can end your season in this format, so avoid stacking starters' byes.
5. **Reserve FAAB expectations, not just draft budget** — you'll want cash on hand for the mid-season talent floods that guillotine eliminations create.

## What to skip vs. the ESPN league's process

- No `dst-expert` or kicker planning — neither is rostered.
- No bye-week-collision check against an *already-drafted* teammate roster the way the ESPN draft manager does round-by-round — instead, check bye clustering within Kelly's own roster as it fills, since there's no round structure to anchor the check to.
- No trade-deadline planning — trades are disabled all season.
