# Positional Value Tiers — Yahoo Guillotine League

Scoring recap: 0.5 PPR for QB/RB/WR, **1.0 PPR for TE**, -1 INT, 6-pt all TDs, standard yardage rates. No K/DST rostered.

## TE — premium tier, budget for it deliberately

A TE catching 90 targets a season earns ~45 pts from receptions alone here — the same target share on an RB/WR earns half that (~22.5 pts). That's a real, structural gap, not a marginal one. Practical effect:

- The true top-tier pass-catching TEs (high target share, high catch rate, red-zone usage) should be valued **closer to a low-end WR1/RB1 than to a typical "TE streamer" price**, even though it's only one roster slot.
- Mid-tier TEs (committee/inconsistent target share) don't get much lift — the premium only matters if the volume is actually there.
- Expect the auction room to bid these up aggressively once the market catches on (Kelly already flagged this pre-draft) — don't get anchored to a half-PPR mental price on a TE.
- If the elite tier goes for more than budget logic supports (see `auction-budget-strategy.md`), it's fine to pivot to volume-based streaming at TE later — but know going in whether you're paying up or punting, rather than drifting into a mediocre mid-price TE that gets neither benefit.

## RB/WR — still the volume positions, but reception value is halved

0.5 PPR softens the gap between a low-target/high-efficiency player and a high-target/low-efficiency one compared to a full-PPR league. Slightly favor:

- Players with real touchdown equity and yardage volume over pure short-target compilers.
- Workhorse-role RBs (goal-line + passing-down work) over committee backs, since the reception cushion that normally props up a limited-touch pass-catching back is worth less here.

Still the two positions to spend the bulk of the budget on — one TE decision doesn't change that RB/WR carry the roster (2 RB + 2 WR + flex = 5 of 7 starting spots).

## QB — mild devaluation, but less than the ESPN league

4-pt passing TD and **-1 INT** (not -2) is closer to standard scoring than the ESPN league's rules — INT-prone volume passers are punished less here. Still just one starting slot in an 18-team league with a deep waiver pool behind it. Don't be first to pay premium QB prices, but don't apply ESPN-league-level QB avoidance either — recalibrate per-league, don't copy the ESPN QB tiers file over.

## No K/DST

Not rostered at all — zero budget planning needed for either. Don't let auction-day habit (throwing $1-2 at a defense late) creep in; there's no roster slot for it.
