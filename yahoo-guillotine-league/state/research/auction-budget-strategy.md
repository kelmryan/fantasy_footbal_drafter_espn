# Auction Budget Strategy — $1000 Budget, 14 Roster Spots

18-team auction, $1000/team, no trades all season. Roster: QB, WR, WR, RB, RB, TE, FLEX, 6 BN, 1 IR (14 spots; IR typically doesn't need to be filled on draft day).

## The floor math

Yahoo enforces a $1 minimum bid per team on any player, and (per standard Yahoo auction rules) a $1 floor reserved per remaining unfilled roster spot. With 13 non-IR spots to fill:

- **Max bid on any single player at the start of the draft** = $1000 − $1 × 12 (remaining spots after this one) = **$988**, in theory — but spending anywhere near that on one player is not a real strategy with 12 more spots to fill.
- Track Kelly's **live max possible bid** throughout the draft: remaining budget − $1 × (unfilled required spots − 1). Never let a recommendation exceed this ceiling.

## Suggested allocation framework (adjust as the room's pricing reveals itself)

- **~60-70% of budget ($600-700) on 3-5 "anchor" players** — a mix of RB/WR studs and, if the TE-premium tier is available at a price that respects its real value (see `positional-value-tiers.md`), one elite pass-catching TE.
- **~20-30% ($200-300) on 4-6 solid starters/flex-caliber depth** — this is where guillotine format pushes you toward balance over stars-and-scrubs; don't starve this tier to stretch for one more star.
- **Remaining ~$100-150 on bench depth at $1-5 each**, prioritizing handcuffs and players with a real path to weekly starting value if injuries/eliminations open volume.

## Nomination tactics

- Nominate players you don't want early to drain other teams' budgets — especially effective on positions where the room seems eager (watch for early TE nominations turning into bidding wars; let others overspend there if you're not in on that specific player).
- Nominate your actual targets when the market looks thin on cash (mid-draft, after a few expensive names have gone) rather than early when everyone's flush.
- If the elite TE tier is clearly going to command star-RB/WR prices, decide in advance whether you're paying up for one or fully punting to stream cheap/committee TEs — don't let the room's momentum pull you into an expensive-but-not-elite TE that gets neither benefit.
