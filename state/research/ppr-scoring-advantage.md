# PPR Scoring Advantage Strategy

_How to leverage full PPR (1 pt/reception) for draft edge_

## Core Principle

**In full PPR, receptions = yards**

- 10 receptions = 10 points (same as 100 yards)
- A player with 8 catches for 60 yards = 14 PPR points
- A player with 3 catches for 100 yards = 13 PPR points
- **Volume > Big plays** in PPR

## PPR Positional Value Shift

### **Standard Scoring Top-5 Positions:**
1. RB (touches + TDs)
2. WR (big plays + TDs)
3. QB (passing volume)
4. TE (TD upside)

### **Full PPR Top-5 Positions:**
1. **WR** (target volume + receptions) ⬆️
2. **Pass-catching RB** (3-down backs, receiving threats) ⬆️
3. **PPR TE** (target hogs like Travis Kelce) ⬆️
4. RB (rushing-only backs) ⬇️
5. QB (unchanged)

**Key insight:** Full PPR **elevates WR** and **pass-catching RBs** above rushing-only RBs.

## Player Archetypes to Target

### **RBs (prioritize these traits):**

✅ **3-down backs** (Bijan Robinson, De'Von Achane, Jahmyr Gibbs)
- On field for passing downs = receptions
- 50+ receptions/season = +50 PPR points over rushing-only backs

✅ **Pass-catching specialists** (even if not lead rusher)
- Example: Jaleel McLaughlin, Tyler Goodson types
- 3-5 catches/game = 3-5 free PPR points

❌ **Avoid: Early-down plodders**
- Goal-line vultures with <20 receptions/year
- They score TDs but lack PPR floor

### **WRs (prioritize these traits):**

✅ **Slot receivers / high-target WRs**
- 8+ targets/game = elite PPR floor
- Examples: Nico Collins, Puka Nacua, Wan'Dale Robinson

✅ **No. 1 WRs on pass-heavy offenses**
- 25%+ target share
- Examples: CeeDee Lamb, Justin Jefferson, Amon-Ra St. Brown

⚠️ **De-prioritize: Deep threats with low target volume**
- DK Metcalf types: 4-5 targets, 80 yards, 1 TD = good game in standard
- Same player in PPR: 4-5 catches, 80 yards, 1 TD = 14-15 points (WR2/WR3 level, not WR1)

### **TEs (prioritize these traits):**

✅ **Elite target-share TEs**
- Travis Kelce, Mark Andrews, Sam LaPorta
- 8+ targets/game = WR-level PPR production

❌ **Avoid: TD-dependent TEs**
- 3 targets/game, 2 catches, 20 yards, 1 TD = 10 points (mediocre)
- Stream these off waivers, don't draft early

## Draft Strategy Adjustments for PPR

### **Early Rounds (1-5):**
- **WR becomes equal value to RB** in Rounds 2-4
  - Standard: RB-RB-RB is common
  - PPR: **RB-WR-WR or WR-WR-RB is viable** (mock draft used RB-RB-WR-WR-TE = balanced)

### **Mid Rounds (6-10):**
- **Target WRs with 6+ targets/game floor**
  - These become your FLEX starters (12-15 PPG floor in PPR)
  - Examples from mock: Wan'Dale Robinson, Khalil Shakir

### **Late Rounds (11-14):**
- **PPR "onesie" WRs > backup RBs**
  - A WR getting 5 catches for 50 yards = 10 PPR points
  - A backup RB getting 8 carries for 35 yards = 3.5 points
  - **PPR rewards WR depth on your bench**

## FLEX Position Strategy (RB/WR/TE)

**In full PPR:**
- **WR in FLEX >> RB in FLEX** (on average)
  - WR3s average 10-12 PPG (6 catches, 60 yards)
  - RB3s average 8-10 PPG (12 carries, 50 yards, 1 catch)

**Mock draft FLEX decision:**
- Tyler Goodson (RB) = 8-10 PPG floor (handcuff upside)
- Wan'Dale Robinson (WR) = 10-12 PPG floor (reception volume)
- **Recommendation: Start Wan'Dale in FLEX, keep Goodson as RB depth**

## Key Metrics for Agents to Reference

When evaluating players in PPR:

**RBs:**
- Receptions/game: 4+ = elite PPR, 2-3 = solid, <2 = avoid unless elite rusher
- Snap % on passing downs: 60%+ = good, <40% = red flag

**WRs:**
- Targets/game: 8+ = elite, 6-7 = solid WR2, <5 = risky
- Target share: 25%+ = WR1, 20-24% = WR2, <15% = WR3/WR4

**TEs:**
- Targets/game: 7+ = elite (worth early pick), 5-6 = solid TE1, <4 = streamable
