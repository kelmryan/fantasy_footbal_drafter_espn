## Draft Strategy Notes

- **QB:** 4-pt passing TD (not 6) devalues QB - don't reach early rounds
- **Kicker:** Highly random, draft last round
- **D/ST:** Non-standard scoring (pts allowed + yards allowed) = stream by matchup, don't draft early
- **Snake at pick 13:** Get picks 13 & 16, then long wait to pick 41 - prioritize filling starter spots early
