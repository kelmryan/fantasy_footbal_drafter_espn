# Core Draft Strategy — click clack (2026)

_Reference this for all draft decisions. Agents should cite specific rules when making recommendations._

## League Context
- **Format:** 14 teams, full PPR (1 pt/reception), snake draft
- **Scoring:** 4-pt passing TD (NOT 6) — **devalues QB significantly**
- **Roster:** 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), 1 D/ST, 1 K, 7 bench
- **No IR slot** — injured players occupy bench spots

## Draft Philosophy by Phase

### **Early Rounds (1-5): Secure Elite Starters**
- **Round 1:** Elite RB or WR. **RB preferred at pick 1** (scarcity, touches, floor)
- **Rounds 2-3 (picks 28-29):** Lock RB2 + WR1, OR WR1 + WR2 in full PPR
  - **RB + WR is safest** (secures positional balance)
  - **WR + WR high-upside** (PPR rewards elite WRs, RB depth available later)
- **Rounds 4-5 (picks 56-57):** Fill remaining starter holes (WR2, RB3, or elite TE if available)

### **Mid Rounds (6-10): Depth + Positional Insurance**
- **QB:** Draft here ONLY if elite tier falls (Josh Allen, Lamar Jackson). Otherwise WAIT.
  - 4-pt passing TDs = QB is not premium position
  - Streaming QB2s is viable in 14-team leagues
- **TE:** If elite TE (Kelce, LaPorta, Andrews) available in Rounds 4-6, consider. Otherwise stream.
- **RB/WR depth:** Target high-floor PPR players (reception volume > TD upside)

### **Late Rounds (11-14): Lottery Tickets + Streaming**
- **Handcuffs:** Prioritize YOUR OWN RB1/RB2 handcuffs (e.g., Bijan's backup, Javonte's backup)
- **Injured stashes:** High-upside injured players (Jonathon Brooks types) if no IR slot conflict
- **D/ST:** **Draft last 2 rounds** — streaming D/ST by matchup >> drafting elite D/ST early
- **K:** **Draft LAST round** — kickers are totally random, zero edge in drafting early

## Value-Based Drafting (BPA vs. Need)

**Use this decision tree:**
1. **Is there an elite player 1+ round ahead of ADP?** → Take BPA, ignore need
2. **Are all available players roughly equal value?** → Fill biggest roster need
3. **Do you have 0 starters at a position with <3 rounds left?** → Fill need NOW (e.g., no QB by Round 12 = problem)

## Risk Management

### **RED FLAGS (avoid unless value is extreme):**
- Age 30+ RBs with injury history (e.g., Christian McCaffrey at 30 with Achilles + PCL)
- WRs with catastrophic QB situations (e.g., Garrett Wilson with no QB)
- Players on injury report in August with "week-to-week" or "day-to-day" status

### **YELLOW FLAGS (discount ADP, but draftable):**
- Minor camp injuries (shoe issues, precautionary rest)
- Age 28-29 RBs (still productive but monitor workload)
- WRs switching teams (need time to build chemistry)

## Full PPR Leverage

**Target players with:**
- **High target share** (25%+ of team targets)
- **Slot/underneath routes** (Wan'Dale Robinson, Khalil Shakir types)
- **Pass-catching RBs** (De'Von Achane, James Cook types)
- **3-down backs** (Bijan Robinson, Jahmyr Gibbs)

**Avoid:**
- TD-dependent WRs with <5 targets/game
- "Boom/bust" deep threats (DK Metcalf types are riskier in PPR than standard)

## Snake Draft Positional Value

**14-team snake = long wait between picks after turnaround**

**Pick 1 strategy:**
- Picks: 1, 28-29, 56-57, 84-85, 112-113, 140-141, 168-169, 196-197
- **After each pick pair, you wait 26 picks** — anticipate runs on positions
- **When on the clock at turnaround (28-29, 56-57, etc.):** Take 2 players in DIFFERENT positions unless elite value (e.g., two top-10 WRs is OK, two RB3s is NOT)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Drafting QB before Round 8 in 4-pt passing TD league (unless Josh Allen falls)
❌ Drafting K before final round
❌ Drafting D/ST before Round 13
❌ Drafting handcuffs for OTHER TEAMS' RBs (only YOUR OWN handcuffs matter)
❌ Ignoring bye weeks and creating Week 11 cluster
❌ Drafting injured player with 8+ week timeline when you have no IR slot
❌ Reaching for "your guy" 2+ rounds early (that's how you lose drafts)

## Agents: How to Use This File

When making a draft recommendation:
1. Check what round we're in → cite the relevant phase strategy
2. Check if it's a turnaround pick (28-29, 56-57, etc.) → cite snake draft strategy
3. Check for red/yellow flags → cite risk management rules
4. Compare BPA vs. need → cite value-based decision tree
5. **Always include the specific rule/principle** in your recommendation (e.g., "Per draft-strategy-core.md, 4-pt passing TDs devalue QB — wait until Round 8+")

This reduces tokens by referencing pre-written strategy instead of regenerating logic each time.
