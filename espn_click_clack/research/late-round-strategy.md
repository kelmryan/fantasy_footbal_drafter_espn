# Late-Round Strategy (Rounds 11-14)

_Maximizing value in the final rounds of the draft_

## Core Principle

**Rounds 11-14 are NOT about starters — they're about:**
1. **Handcuffs** (insurance for YOUR RBs)
2. **Lottery tickets** (injured stashes, breakout candidates)
3. **Streaming positions** (D/ST, K — draft these LAST)

**Do NOT draft "safe" players** — you already have starters. Late rounds = upside hunting.

## Priority Order (Rounds 11-14)

### **1. YOUR OWN RB Handcuffs** (Rounds 11-12)
- **Definition:** The backup RB who becomes a league-winner if your RB1/RB2 gets hurt
- **Mock draft example:** Tyler Goodson (handcuff for Jonathan Taylor, who we don't own, BUT Goodson has standalone value as Colts backup)

**Handcuffs to target:**
- **If you drafted Bijan Robinson** → Brian Robinson Jr (ATL backup, pick 81 in mock)
  - Per rb-intel.md: "No. 2 RB behind Bijan Robinson; handcuff only"
  - Worth drafting Round 11-12 if available
- **If you drafted Javonte Williams** → Hunter Luepke (DAL backup)
  - Goal-line role, insurance if Javonte injured
- **If you drafted De'Von Achane** → Jeff Wilson Jr (MIA backup)

**DO NOT handcuff other teams' RBs** — you want the backup to YOUR starters, not someone else's.

### **2. Injured Stashes / Lottery Tickets** (Rounds 11-13)
- **Definition:** High-upside players who are currently injured but could return mid-season or playoffs
- **Mock draft example:** Jonathon Brooks (RB, CAR, pick 196)
  - Per rb-intel.md: "Out 2 months; high upside lottery ticket"
  - If he returns healthy Week 10-12, he could be a FLEX starter for playoffs

**When to draft an injured player:**
✅ **IF:** Injury timeline = return by Week 10 or earlier (playoff-relevant)
✅ **IF:** Player has standalone RB2/WR2 upside when healthy
❌ **AVOID IF:** No IR slot + injury timeline = out all season (wasted bench spot)

**Other lottery ticket types:**
- **Rookie WRs in ambiguous depth charts** (Quentin Johnston types)
- **Backup QBs on run-heavy teams** (Taysom Hill types in TE-eligible leagues)
- **Post-hype sleepers** (players who flopped last year but have new opportunity)

### **3. Backup QB (Round 12-13)** — ONLY if rostering 2 QBs
- **Mock draft example:** Will Levis (QB, pick 197)
- **Why:** Anthony Richardson has injury history; if he goes down, waiver QB pool is terrible in 14-team league
- **When to skip:** If your QB1 is ultra-durable (Josh Allen types) OR you prefer to stream

**Rule:** Never draft 2 QBs before Round 11 in a 4-pt passing TD league (QB is not premium).

### **4. D/ST (Round 13)**
- **Mock draft: 49ers D/ST (pick 112)**
- **Strategy:** Draft a "good not great" D/ST in Round 13, then **stream by matchup** all season
  - Per CLAUDE.md: "D/ST scoring is non-standard — favors streaming a defense by matchup over drafting one early"
  - Ravens, Steelers, 49ers, Jets = solid "set-and-forget" options, but you'll still stream bad matchup weeks

**DO NOT draft D/ST before Round 13** — positional players have more value.

### **5. K (Round 14 — LAST PICK)**
- **Mock draft: Jake Moody (K, pick 113)**
- **Strategy:** Draft literally any starting kicker with your last pick
- **Why kickers are random:**
  - Kicker scoring is 90% dependent on offense (field position, red zone efficiency)
  - **You cannot predict this** — streaming kickers by matchup is equally effective as drafting early
  - Example: Justin Tucker (elite K) scored fewer PPG than waiver-wire kickers in 2025

**NEVER draft a kicker before your final pick** — it's a waste of roster capital.

## Bench Composition After Draft

**Ideal 7-bench roster:**
1. **RB depth** (1-2 players: handcuff + lottery ticket)
2. **WR depth** (2-3 players: bye week fill-ins, PPR volume plays)
3. **Backup QB** (1 player: injury insurance for QB1)
4. **Injured stash** (1 player: playoff upside)
5. **Empty spot OR extra RB/WR** (1 player: flex depth)

**Mock draft final bench:**
- Jaleel McLaughlin (RB depth)
- Wan'Dale Robinson (WR depth)
- Tank Bigsby (RB depth)
- Khalil Shakir (WR depth)
- Jonathon Brooks (injured stash)
- Will Levis (backup QB)
- *(Empty)* — room to work waivers Week 1

✅ **This is a well-balanced bench** — covers bye weeks, injuries, and has lottery ticket upside.

## Common Late-Round Mistakes

❌ **Drafting "safe" veteran backups** (e.g., Latavius Murray, Damien Harris types)
- These players have ZERO upside — if they become starters, they're RB3/RB4 at best
- **Draft for upside, not safety** in late rounds

❌ **Drafting a second TE "just in case"**
- TEs are streamable off waivers (tons of 5-8 PPG options available)
- Use that bench spot for RB/WR depth instead

❌ **Drafting D/ST or K before Round 13**
- Every round earlier = wasted pick on a streaming position

❌ **Rostering 3 QBs**
- Unless it's a 2QB league (it's not), you only need 1-2 QBs max
- 3rd QB is a wasted bench spot
